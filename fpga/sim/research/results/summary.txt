Issues #13 and #14: summary of fpga/sim/research (iq_harness.py, candidates.py,
experiment.py; compare.txt, quant.txt). Simulation only: a self-contained
channel and score, not C5VRX's research/sim.py, no board IQ. 6 seeds per
condition. Absolute numbers are not comparable with fpga/sim/fading; deltas
between algorithms on the same signal are.

Reproduce:
    python fpga/sim/research/experiment.py 4          # compare.txt (~2 min, 4 cores)
    python fpga/sim/research/experiment.py quant 4    # quant.txt

Caveat on the score: luma SINAD against a full-band truth rewards any loss of
bandwidth (a low-pass removes more noise than signal). Only bandwidth-matched
pairs are conclusive: lag2 vs disc, gated vs plain PLL, split vs split, and the
3 MHz detail column next to every SINAD delta.

#13  multi-timescale phase / frequency estimation on the same 40 MS/s angle
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase-wrap bound: a wrapped difference over L samples is unambiguous for
|f| < FS / (2 L): L = 1: 20 MHz, 2: 10, 4: 5, 8: 2.5 MHz. The test video spans
about -1.6 .. +4.0 MHz (sync tip .. white) plus chroma; with noise L = 4 has
~0.5 MHz margin, L = 8 has none.
Unwrapped, a lag-L difference / L is the mean of the L one-sample differences
(a 100 ns boxcar for L = 4): the only new information would be slips that
complete inside L samples and wrap away.

Answer to #13's question: on this model the longer windows do not extract
more weak-signal information than the PLL / D3 path. Near threshold the
differences come from the loop and its bandwidth; the repeatable gain is
precision at strong signals (the 10-bit angle of the 50 ns discriminator).
